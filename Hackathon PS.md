# Technology-Enabled Food Waste Intelligence & Redistribution Platform

Large quantities of edible food are wasted daily by restaurants, institutions, and event venues, while nearby communities and shelters face ongoing food shortages. 

Current donation efforts are often informal and poorly coordinated, lacking real-time visibility, structured matching, and safety mechanisms. This leads to inefficiencies, delayed pickups, and potential food safety concerns. 

**Objective**

Design a technology-enabled platform that:

- Allows donors to report surplus food in real time
- Intelligently matches donations with nearby verified recipients
- Optimizes collection and redistribution timelines
- Ensures traceability and basic quality checks 
- Measures and displays social impact

The goal is to create a reliable, transparent, and efficient food redistribution ecosystem.

**Must Include**

Your solution must include:
- Role-Based Access (Donor / Recipient / Admin)
- Real-time surplus reporting system
- Intelligent matching based on location, quantity, and urgency
- Status tracking (reported —> accepted —> picked up —> delivered)
- Basic verification and trust indicators
- Impact dashboard (e.g., meals saved, waste reduced)
- Mobile-responsive design
